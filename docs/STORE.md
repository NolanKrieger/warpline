# Warpline — store page draft (for Nolan to edit and approve)

**Short description (≤ 300 chars)**
A 2D portal speedrun platformer. Two portals, one rule: speed in = speed out. Fling across spike pits, loop a fall until nothing falls faster, bounce, slide and ride your way through 20 levels — then chase the developer's ghost, beat every medal, and build and share your own levels.

**About this game**
Warpline is a precision platformer about momentum. Your little robot carries a two-portal gun, and every portal keeps your speed exactly: fall into one, fly out of the other. Drop down a shaft and come out of a wall at fifty tiles a second. Put one portal under your feet and one above your head and fall until you hit terminal velocity. Run across speed gel and turn it all into height.

- **20 hand-built levels** that mix puzzles, precise platforming and pure flow — lasers you redirect through portals, turrets whose bullets press buttons for you, timed doors, bounce and speed gel, moving and orbiting panels that carry your portals.
- **Built for speedrunning** — an exact in-game timer that starts on your first input, instant restart, splits and sum of best for full-game runs, deterministic replays.
- **Race ghosts** — your personal best, or the developer's route, running alongside you.
- **Medals** — bronze, silver, gold and the developer time on every level.
- **Level editor** — build your own levels with every mechanic in the game, clear them once to set an author time, and share them as a copy-paste code.
- **Clean, readable art** — red always kills, cyan and magenta are always portals, and nothing depends on colour alone.
